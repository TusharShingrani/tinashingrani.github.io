Who I am

About Me.

TS

I'm a Communication & Multimedia Design (CMD) student at Hogeschool Rotterdam, specialising in UX/UI design. I'm passionate about making technology feel intuitive, accessible, and genuinely enjoyable to use.

My design process always starts with people — I conduct user research, build personas, map journeys, sketch wireframes, and iterate through prototypes until the experience feels just right.

Outside of my studies I love exploring visual design trends, experimenting with micro-interactions, and finding inspiration in everyday objects.
